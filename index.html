How to play

  1. 1

    You and the chicken take turns hitting the cup. Hit TAP on your beat, when the ring closes on the cup.

  2. 2

    If the chicken snatches the cup, the bell is open. Don't tap. Hit the bell and you get smacked.

  3. 3

    On your beat, hit TAKE to steal the cup. If the chicken slams the bell, everything freezes and you get to SMACK it. Put the cup back on your next beat.

  4. 4

    The longer you play it straight, the lower the chicken's suspicion and the more likely it falls for a grab.

  5. 5

    Miss your beat and you get smacked too. Win the level and you get to finish the chicken off. Every level is faster.

Keyboard: Space tap · F take · Esc pause. In the finisher, hold either button.
