Privacy Policy

Cluck Around and Find Out · Last updated October 8, 2026

Cluck Around and Find Out ("the game") is made by an independent developer. This page explains what the game does with your information. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. It does not send your information to the developer or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, eggs, items, finishers and settings) is saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your device. Deleting the app, or clearing the website's data in your browser, deletes it.

Network use

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub receives standard technical information (such as your IP address) when your device downloads the game. See GitHub's privacy statement. The web version may also load its fonts from Google Fonts; see Google's privacy policy. The installed app keeps a copy of the game on your device so it can run offline.

Purchases

If the game offers in-app purchases in an app store version, payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. The developer never sees your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting data (for example, if ads or leaderboards are added), this page will be updated before that change ships, and the date above will change.
